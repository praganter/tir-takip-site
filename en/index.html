How it works

Setup takes a minute. The phone on the windscreen watches the trailer ahead while you sleep in the back.

  1. Mount the phone

    Fix it to the windscreen, tap "Select target" and fit the yellow box to the back of the trailer.

  2. Set the alarm and rest

    The screen dims and the phone keeps watching the vehicle ahead. Leave the rest to it.

  3. Wake up when it moves

    The alarm sounds at full volume the moment the vehicle ahead pulls away. Hold for 1 second to stop it.

When it rings

Not only when the queue moves, but whenever monitoring is at risk.

  • The truck ahead moves off

    At full volume, even in silent mode.

  • The camera loses the trailer

    When the windscreen fogs up or the phone shifts.

  • The phone overheats

    A phone baking in the sun warns you before it shuts down.

On iPhone and iPad, monitoring runs while the app is on screen: don't lock the screen or leave the app.

How do I not lose my place in a truck queue while I sleep?

Mount your phone on the windscreen so the rear camera sees the truck ahead. Truck Watch watches the vehicle ahead and sounds a loud alarm the moment it moves, so you can rest without losing your place.

Does it need internet? Does it work abroad?

Monitoring works offline; images are processed on the phone. You don't need roaming at the border or abroad. Connecting once a week is enough to verify your subscription.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later, and Android 10 or later. On iPhone and iPad, monitoring runs while the app is on screen.

Why didn't the alarm ring?

Make sure the phone is charging, the volume is up and the yellow box sits on the trailer. The "Before you start" list in the app shows what is missing.

Does it work at night or in fog?

Darkness, fog or a dirty windscreen can weaken the image. If the camera loses sight of the trailer, the alarm rings to warn you.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period.
